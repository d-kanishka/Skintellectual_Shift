# Skintellectual Shift: Understanding Consumer Trust in Cosmetic Brand Marketing

 It is a Business Analytics case study that investigates consumer trust and purchase intent in the cosmetic and skincare market. The study focuses on comparing two marketing approaches: celebrity/influencer endorsements and ingredient-transparency marketing.

The primary dataset consists of 103 genuine responses collected through Google Forms. Additional synthetic records were generated using NVIDIA NeMo Data Designer based on patterns observed in the collected responses to support the analytical modelling process.

The study involves data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, and predictive modelling using Logistic Regression and Decision Tree classification. The analysis focuses on identifying factors associated with consumer purchase intent and understanding how different forms of marketing influence consumer trust.
